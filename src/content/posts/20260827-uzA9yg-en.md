<h2>Let&rsquo;s Rediscover &ldquo;Motorsport&rdquo;</h2>
<p>Motorsport has never been just about being &ldquo;fast.&rdquo;</p>
<p>When a race car crosses the finish line, we only see a few minutes — sometimes a few seconds — of the race. What we rarely notice is the driver&rsquo;s judgement behind the wheel, the engineers&rsquo; tuning of the car, the team&rsquo;s calculations on tires and fuel, and the countless tests and failures along the way.</p>
<p>From F1, WEC and WRC, to formula racing, touring cars, GT, endurance racing and rallying; from a car&rsquo;s aerodynamic design, to overtaking, defending, pit stops and tire strategy on track —</p>
<p>Motorsport itself is a discipline that blends mechanics, engineering, physics, mathematics, management and teamwork.</p>
<p><strong>And opening that door is exactly what DHGT wants to do.</strong></p>
<h2>Start With a Race, Get to Know the Automotive World</h2>
<p>Maybe you once stayed up late to watch an F1 race.</p>
<p>Maybe you love the lights of Le Mans, the dust of rallying, the roar of GT cars — or maybe you simply love a beautiful line on a car.</p>
<p>You might ask:</p>
<p>Why are race cars designed the way they are?</p>
<p>Why do they have front wings, diffusers and such complex aerodynamics?</p>
<p>Why does every circuit demand a completely different setup?</p>
<p>Why does the same car perform so differently in different drivers&rsquo; hands?</p>
<p>Why does a team with the faster car sometimes still lose the race?</p>
<p><strong>These questions are exactly what makes motorsport so fascinating.</strong></p>
<p><strong>DHGT hopes to take everyone from &ldquo;watching the race&rdquo; to &ldquo;understanding the race.&rdquo;</strong></p>
<hr>
<h2>Why Did We Found DHGT?</h2>
<p>In 2025, Donghua DHGT Motorsport Club (Dongcheng) was officially established.</p>
<p><strong>It is a new departure — and also a continuation.</strong></p>
<p>We want to bring the motorsport culture that once belonged to the Ecological Park campus to the Dongcheng campus, giving more students the chance to experience racing, learn about the automotive industry, and get to know automotive culture — and to let this passion for speed and machinery keep growing on a new campus.</p>
<p>What we want to build is not just a club for &ldquo;talking about cars.&rdquo;</p>
<p>We hope this can become a space that truly belongs to car enthusiasts:</p>
<p>Some people discover F1 here for the first time;<br>some start learning about race engineering because of an endurance race;<br>some love machinery and begin studying how cars are built;<br>some love photography and capture every moment on track;<br>some love history and trace the timeline of the automotive industry;<br>and some walk in simply because of one sentence — &ldquo;I like cars.&rdquo;</p>
<p>Our interests may differ, our directions may differ,<br>but passion always brings us together.</p>
<p>So through race sharing, automotive culture exchange, knowledge sessions, themed discussions, visual documentation and club activities, we want more students to understand motorsport, get to know the automotive industry, and learn about cars — and to help everyone who truly loves cars find like-minded friends here.</p>
<p><strong>From Ecological Park to Dongcheng.</strong></p>
<p><strong>The circuit is different, but the passion is unchanged.</strong></p>
<p><strong>DHGT, we race as one.</strong></p>
<hr>
<h2>What&rsquo;s Next for DHGT?</h2>
<p>Going forward, we will keep sharing on our channel:</p>
<p><strong>Racing events</strong><br>From F1, WRC and GT to many more series — the stories behind the races.</p>
<p><strong>The automotive industry</strong><br>Car brands, classic models, key technologies and the history of the industry.</p>
<p><strong>Racing technology</strong><br>Engines, aerodynamics, suspension, tires, car setup and vehicle dynamics.</p>
<p><strong>Race strategy</strong><br>Pit stops, tires, attack and defense, weather and team decisions — studied together.</p>
<p><strong>Automotive culture</strong><br>From race cars to road cars, from classic legends to modern trends — exploring what automotive culture can be.</p>
<p><strong>Club news</strong><br>Recording DHGT&rsquo;s activities, member stories, conversations and every moment worth keeping.</p>
<hr>
<h2>In Closing</h2>
<p>Perhaps we can&rsquo;t make everyone a racing driver.</p>
<p>But we can help more people truly understand:</p>
<p>why a car deserves to be loved.</p>
<p>A race car can be a crystallization of mechanics and engineering;</p>
<p>a race can be a contest of strategy and courage;</p>
<p>and a club can bring together strangers through a shared passion.</p>
<p>2025, DHGT Dongcheng.</p>
<p>This time, we are not just chasing speed.</p>
<p>We want to understand the world behind the speed.</p>
<p><strong>Welcome to Donghua DHGT Motorsport Club (Dongcheng).</strong></p>
<p><strong>Welcome to our circuit.</strong></p>
