<p>2025 Formula 1 World Drivers&rsquo; Champion Lando Norris has signed a contract extension with the McLaren Mastercard Formula 1 Team that keeps him at the team until at least 2030, with options to extend for multiple years beyond that date.</p>
<p>Lando Norris, 26, from Somerset, England, is a homegrown British talent. He joined McLaren through the team&rsquo;s Young Driver Programme in 2017 as a test and simulator driver. He was promoted to a full race seat in 2019 and has since started 163 Grands Prix for the team — the most of any driver in McLaren&rsquo;s history. In that time he has taken 18 pole positions, 48 podium finishes, 13 Grand Prix victories, two Constructors&rsquo; Championships and one Drivers&rsquo; World Championship.</p>
<p>Beyond confirming that Lando will remain with McLaren for at least the next four years, he will also be gifted an MCL39 — the very car he drove during his 2025 title-winning season.</p>
<p>The gift will forever commemorate his contribution to the team, in particular his role in securing McLaren&rsquo;s second consecutive Constructors&rsquo; Championship in 2025, while he also became the team&rsquo;s first Formula 1 World Drivers&rsquo; Champion since 2008.</p>
