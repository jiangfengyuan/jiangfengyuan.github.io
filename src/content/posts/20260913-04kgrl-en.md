<p>Qualifying for the 2026 F1 Spanish Grand Prix has concluded at Madrid&rsquo;s Madring circuit. McLaren driver Lando Norris set a 1:31.824 on his final Q3 lap, beating Mercedes driver Antonelli by just 0.011 seconds to take the first pole position in F1 history at this brand-new track.<br>From a gearbox problem on Friday to a near-perfect lap on Saturday, Norris put himself on the front row with a lap for the ages. Verstappen took third, with Hamilton and Leclerc fourth and fifth — the top four come from four different teams, setting up a mouth-watering start to the race.</p>

<h2>Key Takeaways</h2>
<ul>
<li>Norris and Antonelli were separated by just 11 milliseconds; Antonelli will attack Norris from the front row in the race.</li>
<li>Hamilton led Leclerc by only 0.006 seconds, with both Ferraris right behind the leaders.</li>
